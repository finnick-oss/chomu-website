Chomu is a no-frills hiragana and katakana quiz app. Pick a category, answer 10 quick multiple-choice questions, and see your score instantly. No account, no ads, no tracking.

つかいかた

Three taps to your first quiz.

01Pick a category

Hiragana or Katakana.

Choose which kana set you want to practice. Each one covers the full gojūon set of 46 characters.

02Answer 10 questions

Quick multiple-choice.

See a kana character, pick the correct romaji from four options. Instant visual feedback after every answer.

03See your score

Instant results.

Get your percentage and correct count right away, then try again to beat your best.

04Track progress

All stored on your device.

Your best scores and quiz count are saved locally — no account needed, and you can delete it all with one tap, anytime.
